## Hardware Devices Include:
- Two shooter motors.
  - These will run at the same speeds
  - These actually fire the fuel.
- One kicker motor.
  - Push the fuel up from the indexer to the shooter.
- Two indexer motors.
  - Move the fuel from the hopper to the kicker and shooter motors.
- (Potentially) one hood motor.
  - Lowers a hood above the shooter to launch the fuel at a flatter angle for herding.

## Subsystem Hardware Capabilities:
- The hood should control launch angle.
- The shooter speed should control distance.

## Other Notes:
- Indexer also known as feeder.
- Bot shoots toward intake side.
- Indexer motors will run opposite from one another
